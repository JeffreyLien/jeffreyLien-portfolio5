# Business Requirements

## Business problem
Commercial Operations needs a repeatable view of portfolio performance that connects operating behavior, market conditions, forecasting, and commercial outcomes. Analysts should be able to move from an executive portfolio view to asset-level root-cause analysis without redefining metrics each time.

## Users
- Commercial Operations leadership
- Portfolio / asset analysts
- Operations stakeholders
- Data and reporting teams

## Decisions supported
1. Which assets and technologies are driving portfolio margin?
2. Where are availability, capacity factor, or curtailment affecting performance?
3. How closely are forecasts tracking realized generation?
4. During which simulated hours did positive economic spreads coincide with unused operating headroom?
5. Which metrics should be standardized for recurring monthly reporting?

## Functional requirements
- Monthly portfolio scorecard.
- Asset drill-down for operational and commercial KPIs.
- Market-price capture analysis.
- Forecast accuracy reporting.
- Data-quality checks before KPI publication.
- Reusable semantic measures for Power BI.

## Non-functional requirements
- Reproducible analysis.
- Synthetic, non-proprietary data.
- Clear metric definitions.
- Traceable calculations from raw fields to executive KPIs.
