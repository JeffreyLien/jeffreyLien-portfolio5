# Executive Findings

This synthetic six-month portfolio produced **4.40 million MWh** and **$68.8 million** in modeled gross margin.

## Key observations
- **Riverbend CCGT** produced the highest modeled gross margin at approximately **$17.6M**.
- Portfolio hourly availability averaged **98.6%** across all assets.
- The June portfolio forecast model achieved **11.9% MAPE** with MAE of approximately **2,579 MWh/day**.
- The simplified opportunity screen identified approximately **$2.3M** of *modeled* incremental margin potential in periods where thermal operating headroom coincided with attractive economic spreads.
- The opportunity metric is deliberately conservative and should be treated as a prioritization flag, not a dispatch recommendation.

## Recommended next analyses
1. Add start costs, ramp constraints, minimum run time, and hedge positions to opportunity economics.
2. Segment forecast accuracy by technology, weather regime, and price volatility.
3. Add outage-cause codes to distinguish planned maintenance from forced reliability events.
4. Implement governed monthly scorecards in a shared semantic model.
